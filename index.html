Detect AI images using basic geometry

about fluchtpunkt

Based upon the research of Hany Farid, images created by AI lack a common vanishing point while real photographs always converge to a common point. fluchtpunkt is build upon OpenCV's hough transform to extract the dominant straight lines in an image and an algorithm consisting of a RANSAC-based estimator refined by SVD to find the point most of them agree on (vanishing point). If no clear vanishing point exists, the image is most likely AI generated.

motivation

In todays day and age, the internet is filled with AI-Slop (Ai generated texts, images, audio, etc.). While big corporations have proprietary tools to seperate AI-generated content from real one, it is impossible for the average human to differentiate between a real picture and an AI-generated one. I believe AI should be used to cover repetitive tasks, making better and faster decisions and helping humans focus on what really matters: Arts, Connections, Family, Friends, Life in general. As an analogue photographer, I want to make sure that pictures keep their human flair, especially the one that is based on human errors.
